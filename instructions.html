Instructions

Setup

Number of Laps

Enter the number of laps to be run.

Example: 3200 m outdoors = 8 laps.

Highest Hip Number

Enter the highest competitor hip number in the race. Runners are numbered from 1 through this number.

Missing hip numbers or scratches do not need to be removed. Simply never enter those runners. They will remain unfinished and can be ignored when the race is complete.

Race Screen

During the race there are two main jobs:

  1. Start the clock.
  2. Enter runners' hip numbers as they pass.

Starting the Clock

Press START CLOCK to begin the race.

Entering a hip number before the clock is started does not start the race.

Times are recorded when entries are made. When all runners have finished, the clock stops and the finishing order and times are available.

Entering Hip Numbers

Hip numbers can be entered three ways:

  1. Voice Recognition
  2. External Keypad
  3. On-Screen Keypad

All three methods record the same race information and can be used interchangeably.

1. Voice Recognition

Voice Recognition is usually the fastest way to enter runners during a busy race.

Say the runner's hip number followed immediately by the word “comma.”

Example: “12 comma”

“Comma” acts as the Enter key. Lap App waits for that word before accepting the number.

In noisy environments, a close-talking wired microphone can greatly reduce unwanted background sound. It is especially useful when Voice Feedback is being played through an external Bluetooth speaker.

2. External Keypad

A Bluetooth or wired numerical keypad can provide very fast and reliable entry after a little practice.

It lets the operator keep their eyes on the race while keeping their fingers on the keys.

When an external keypad is detected, the on-screen keypad is automatically hidden.

3. On-Screen Keypad

If no external keypad is connected, the on-screen keypad is available.

For busy races, it can be helpful to have one person call out hip numbers while another enters them.

Special Entries

Correcting an Accidental Entry

If a runner is accidentally entered an extra time, enter that runner's hip number as a negative number to add the lap back.

Example: -12

Negative-number corrections are available from the keypad.

Entering Zero for the Pack

Enter 0 when the runners are together in one pack.

A zero records one crossing for the pack and is especially useful early in a race before the field spreads out.

As the race separates, enter individual hip numbers.

Feedback

Feedback confirms that a valid entry was recorded.

Beep Feedback gives a short audible confirmation.

Voice Feedback announces the runner's hip number and laps to go.

Beep Feedback and Voice Feedback can be used separately or together.

Voice Feedback

Voice Feedback announces messages such as:

“Number 12 has 3 to go.”

When the first runner reaches one lap to go, Lap App sounds the bell before announcing the runner's final lap.

If Voice Feedback and Beep Feedback are both enabled, the confirmation beep sounds after the spoken feedback.

Listen During Voice Feedback

Normally, Voice Recognition ignores speech while Lap App is speaking. This helps prevent the app from recognizing its own feedback.

Listen During Voice Feedback keeps recognition active while feedback is playing.

This works best when feedback is sent to a remote Bluetooth speaker and a close-talking microphone is used. The operator can continue calling hip numbers while earlier feedback catches up.

Audio Output

When Voice Feedback is enabled, Audio Output lets you select the speaker used for spoken feedback and the bell.

This is useful when using an external Bluetooth speaker.

Laps to Go

The race table shows each runner's current laps to go.

The displayed number is the lap count you would tell the runner before entering that runner's hip number.

Voice Feedback can announce this information through an external speaker so runners can hear their laps remaining.

Lap Times

Lap Times records the lap time history for every runner.

This provides an audit trail that can help identify a missed entry or an accidental extra entry.

Lap times are also included in exported race data.

Export Results

Race results can be exported as a CSV file for saving or further processing.

Reset

RESET clears the current race so a new race can be started.
