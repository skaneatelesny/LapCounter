The Lap App

Lap counting and race tracking for multi-lap running events

The Lap App helps officials track runners through multi-lap races while keeping attention on the race itself.

Enter runner hip numbers by voice, external keypad, or the on-screen keypad. The app keeps track of laps remaining, records lap times, builds the finishing order, and can provide audible feedback during the race.

  • Voice Recognition for rapid hands-free hip-number entry
  • External keypad and on-screen keypad support
  • Automatic laps-to-go tracking for every runner
  • Pack entry using zero when runners are still together
  • Voice and beep feedback
  • Bell-lap announcement
  • Lap Times history for checking missed or extra entries
  • Finishing order and race times
  • CSV export of race results
