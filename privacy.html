Privacy Policy

The Lap App does not collect personal information for advertising, analytics, tracking, or marketing purposes.

The app does not maintain user accounts and does not send race results or exported race data to the developer.

Voice Recognition

If you enable Voice Recognition, The Lap App uses Apple's Speech Recognition services to convert spoken hip numbers into text.

Speech Recognition requires permission to use the microphone and Apple's Speech Recognition service. Depending on the device and system configuration, audio used for speech recognition may be processed by Apple, including on Apple servers.

The Lap App does not independently record, store, or retain microphone audio. The developer does not receive your speech recordings or recognized speech.

Race Data

Race information such as lap counts, lap times, finishing order, and results is used by the app to operate the race and create exported results.

The Lap App does not transmit this race data to the developer.

Analytics and Tracking

The Lap App does not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Apple Services

Use of Apple's Speech Recognition services is subject to Apple's privacy policies and system privacy controls.

You can control microphone and Speech Recognition permissions in the iPhone or iPad Settings app.
